About Me

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Professors Kaiyu Guan and Bin Peng. My research focuses on developing advanced machine learning approaches to investigate sustainable agricultural systems.
I received my Ph.D. in Biosystems Engineering from Zhejiang University, where I was advised by Professors K.C. Ting, Yibin Ying, and Tao Lin. Following that, I completed my first postdoctoral training at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities with Professor Zhenong Jin.

Research Interests

  • Agroecosystem Modeling: Knowledge-guided AI to study how climate change and human activities affect hydrology and water quality through soil-water-nutrient interactions across agricultural landscapes.
  • Digital Agriculture: Multi-scale sensing (from satellite to in situ) and modeling for cropland monitoring, crop yield prediction, and agro-product quality assessment.
